ReelVarta Local Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Contact: reelvarta@gmail.com.

ReelVarta Local is a private desktop tool operated by Reel Varta for its own news workflow. It accesses the Reel Varta Google account only after the account owner authorizes it.

Google data accessed and used

The tool has Gmail read-only access. It searches the owner's inbox for self-addressed ReelVarta NEWS and REVIEW messages, reads the news topic from the subject, and reads the first line of a review message to determine whether the current draft is approved or needs revision. It does not send, delete, or modify email.

The tool has Google Sheets edit access. It writes story, review, and publication records to Reel Varta's private workflow Sheet. After approval, it writes a news card to the separate Sheet used by the public Reel Varta website.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and workflow state are stored on the owner's laptop. The tool sends the news topic and retrieved news article text to Google's Gemini API to prepare a draft. It does not send the full Gmail message to Gemini. Only after the owner approves a draft does the tool publish the resulting news content to Reel Varta's Facebook Page, Instagram account, public website feed, and a public image URL hosted on GitHub. Private emails and OAuth credentials are not published.

The tool does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Access is limited to this owner-operated workflow. The owner can revoke Google access in their Google Account settings and delete local files in the tool's data folder. The owner can also remove workflow records from the private Sheet and public cards from the website Sheet.
