ReelVarta Local

ReelVarta Local is a private desktop tool used by Reel Varta to research Marathi news topics, prepare drafts for human review, and publish approved posts to the linked Facebook Page, Instagram account, and website news feed.

The tool reads only self-addressed messages with ReelVarta NEWS or REVIEW subjects from the Reel Varta Gmail account. It can update Reel Varta's Google Sheets for private workflow records and approved public website cards. It cannot send email. Publication requires an explicit approval message for the current draft version.

It runs on the owner's laptop when started manually. It is not offered to the public as an app or service.
